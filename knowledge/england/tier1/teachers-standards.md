# Teachers' Standards and professional context

## The standards

Part One — teaching:

1. Set high expectations which inspire, motivate and challenge pupils
2. Promote good progress and outcomes by pupils
3. Demonstrate good subject and curriculum knowledge
4. Plan and teach well-structured lessons
5. Adapt teaching to respond to the strengths and needs of all pupils
6. Make accurate and productive use of assessment
7. Manage behaviour effectively to ensure a good and safe learning environment
8. Fulfil wider professional responsibilities

Part Two covers personal and professional conduct.

They apply to all teachers from the award of QTS and are the framework for appraisal.

## Early career teachers

Induction lasts two years, supported by the Early Career Teacher Entitlement — renamed from the
Early Career Framework in September 2025 and folded into the initial teacher training and early
career framework. Timetable reductions of 10% in year one and 5% in year two are retained; mentor
training is now one year rather than two.

## Directed time and PPA

Teachers' pay and conditions are set annually in the School Teachers' Pay and Conditions Document.
Teachers at maintained schools are directed for up to 1,265 hours over 195 days. **Planning,
preparation and assessment time is guaranteed at a minimum of 10% of timetabled teaching time.**

Do not quote specific pay figures or ranges without checking the current year's document — they
change every September.
