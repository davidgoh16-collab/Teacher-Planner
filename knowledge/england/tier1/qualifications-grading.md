# Qualifications and grading in England

## GCSE

Graded **9 to 1**, 9 highest. Reformed GCSEs are linear: assessed by terminal examination, with
non-exam assessment only where a subject requires it.

- **Grade 4** — standard pass
- **Grade 5** — strong pass (the benchmark in school performance measures)
- **Grade 7** — broadly the old A
- **Grade 9** — above the old A*, awarded to a small proportion of entries

Never describe a grade 4 as a "fail". English and maths must be continued post-16 by students who
have not reached grade 4.

## A level

Graded **A\* to E**, with U for unclassified. Linear, assessed at the end of the course. AS levels
are separate qualifications and no longer count towards the A level.

## Vocational and technical

**BTEC** — graded Pass, Merit, Distinction and Distinction\*, at Level 2 (GCSE-equivalent) and
Level 3 (A-level-equivalent). Assessed through a mix of internal assignments and external units.

**T Levels** — two-year Level 3 programmes with a substantial industry placement, graded Pass,
Merit, Distinction, Distinction\*.

**Functional Skills** — Entry Level 1–3 and Levels 1–2, pass/fail, often taken alongside vocational
study.

## Regulation

**Ofqual** regulates qualifications, sets standards and maintains the Regulated Qualifications
Framework. **JCQ** publishes the shared administrative rules exam officers work to: instructions
for conducting examinations, access arrangements, malpractice procedures and key dates.

Use British terms. Never use GPA, freshman/sophomore, or letter grades A–F for GCSE.
