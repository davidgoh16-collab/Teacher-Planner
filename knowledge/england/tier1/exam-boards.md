# Exam boards and the exam year

## The boards in England

- **AQA** — aqa.org.uk
- **Pearson Edexcel** — qualifications.pearson.com
- **OCR** — ocr.org.uk
- **WJEC Eduqas** — eduqas.co.uk

A school chooses a board per subject, so two departments in the same school routinely sit different
specifications. Never assume which board a teacher uses — ask, or read it from their configuration.

## The shape of the year

Written examinations run from mid-May to late June. Non-exam assessment deadlines fall earlier and
are set by the board per specification. Results are published in August: A level and Level 3
vocational results on the third Thursday, GCSE results the following Thursday. Post-results services
— reviews of marking, access to scripts, appeals — follow, with deadlines published by JCQ.

November resits are available in GCSE English language and mathematics.

## Where the authoritative dates live

Each board publishes its own timetable and key dates, and JCQ publishes the shared administrative
deadlines. **Never state a specific exam date from memory.** Point the teacher at their board's key
dates page, or fetch it, and say when you looked.
